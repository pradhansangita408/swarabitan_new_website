Our Story

The story behind Swarabitan

Swarabitan is built around a simple idea: music should not only be listened to, it should be experienced, understood and passed from one generation to another.

Bengali music has a beautiful heritage filled with Rabindra Sangeet, Nazrul Geeti, folk traditions, devotional music and countless musical stories.

Our vision is to bring this heritage closer to today's students, teachers, artists and music lovers through a modern digital platform.
